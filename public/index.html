Sudoku Solver

Enter any Sudoku. Get the solution.

How to use

  • Click a cell or navigate with arrow keys to select.
  • Enter numbers 1–9 via keyboard or click the number pad below.
  • Use Backspace/Delete or Erase button to clear a cell.
  • Click Solve Sudoku to solve the puzzle.
  • Check entries for conflicts any time.
  • Use Reset to remove solver answers but keep clues.
  • Clear All empties the grid.
  • Undo reverses your last entry.
